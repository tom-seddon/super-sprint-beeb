Brief instructions:

For Super Sprint, use one of the super-sprint disk images.

* {{game_ssd}} (DFS)
* {{game_adl}} (ADFS L)

For Super Sprint (Time Attack), use one of the super-sprint-ta disk
images.

* {{ta_ssd}} (DFS)
* {{ta_adl}} (ADFS L)

Load disk image into a BBC Master/Compact or emulator, and press
SHIFT+BREAK to boot. (Note that high scores are saved to disk, so
please ensure the disk is write enabled!)
